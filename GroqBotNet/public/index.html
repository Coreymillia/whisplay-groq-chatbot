Room monitor controls

Leave start and stop blank to run all day. Use Off to disable the room monitor. Auto brightness lifts dark captures automatically. Oldest captures are deleted automatically to protect the selected free-space reserve.

AI image archive

Imported AI images are copied from Whisplay into a pinned local archive and are never auto-deleted. If storage drops below the free-space reserve, new imports stop instead of deleting archived AI art.
